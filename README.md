# The-floor-is-up-Roblox
(In development)

[![Play Here!](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play_Here-ROBLOX-000000?style=for-the-badge&logo=roblox&logoColor=white)](https://www.roblox.com/games/99475103190855/The-floor-is-up)

**The Floor is Up!** is a fast-paced 3D gravity-flipping auto-runner on roblox. It is inspired by a mobile multiplayer minigame I used to play with my friends.

---

## 🎮 How the game works:
- **Auto run:** You cannot stop. Your character sprints forward automatically, You dodge obstacles by flipping gravity.
- **Flip Gravity:** Press **[E]** to flip gravity quickly and avoid running into obstacles or falling into the void.
- **Dash ability:** Press **[F]** to smash through (some) obstacles.
- **Camera Modes:** There are options for camera angles:
  - 🟦 **2.5D:** Side-scrolling view like the original game.
  - 🧊 **3D:** A angled view down the corridor.
  - 📐 **Isometric:** Another angled view.

---

## 🏆 Lobby & Progression:
- **Lobby area:** Has a shop area, leaderboard area and a level selection area.
- **Gravity Shop:** Spend in-game coins on cool particle trails and flip effects.
